Why Choose Garden Decking?

Garden decking is one of the best ways to make your outdoor space more useful and inviting. A new deck can:

  • Create an outdoor dining or BBQ area
  • Provide a safe, level space for children to play
  • Add value to your home
  • Make a sloped or uneven garden easier to use
  • Improve access to your garden

Decking is flexible. You can choose different shapes, sizes, levels, and finishes to match your style and needs. It's a smart way to get more from your garden all year round.

Garden Decking Service: Our Process

At ARC Sussex Carpentry, we offer complete garden decking services in Brighton and Sussex.

01

Free Consultation

We visit your home, talk about your ideas, measure your garden, and discuss what you want from your new deck.

02

Design and Planning

We design a deck that suits your garden's shape, slope, and size. We consider things like:

  • Built-in seating
  • Planters
  • Lighting options

We'll help you choose materials—hardwood, softwood, or composite decking—and finishes that work best for your budget and Brighton's weather.

03

Clear Quoting

We provide a clear, honest quote with no hidden costs. You'll know exactly what you're paying for.

04

Professional Carpentry Build

Our team of skilled carpenters in Brighton handles all building work. We focus on:

  • Strong, safe, level foundations

Built for Brighton's Climate

Brighton is beautiful, but the coastal air can be harsh on timber.

We help you choose the right materials and treatments to stand up to:

  • Moisture and rain
  • Salt-laden sea air
  • Hot summer sun

We offer timber options like pressure-treated softwood for affordability and durability, or hardwood for strength and beauty. We also build with composite decking, which resists rot and needs less maintenance.
