Carpentry Services Across Sussex

At ARC Sussex Carpentry, we specialise in a full range of carpentry services in Sussex, combining skill, precision, and creativity to bring your vision to life. Our expertise includes loft conversions, bespoke garden rooms, and custom-built decking, as well as constructing timber frame structures, internal studwork, and partition walls. We also design and install staircases and balustrades , lay all types of flooring including real wood, engineered, and laminate, and build fitted wardrobes and shelving to maximise space. Our team handles custom joinery and intricate woodwork, fits cladding, fascias, and soffits, and expertly constructs roof structures and joists. Whether it's doors, skirting, or architraves , no detail is overlooked.

We're committed to delivering tailored solutions for every home—whether you're renovating a period property, extending a modern build, or upgrading your outdoor living space. Wherever you're located in Sussex, you can rely on ARC Sussex Carpentry for honest advice, transparent quotes, and excellent results.
